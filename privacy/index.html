Privacy

FridgeNote · last updated 2 October 2026

FridgeNote is a shared note board for two people. This page describes everything it stores and everything it sends anywhere.

No account, no identity

There is no sign-up. The app creates an anonymous Firebase account, which is a random identifier with no name, email or phone number attached. We never ask for any of those. That identifier is still an identifier: it ties your notes to one install, and it is not anonymity in the everyday sense.

Your notes are encrypted before they leave the phone

Note text and the display names you choose are encrypted on your device with a key generated on your phone. The key is shared with your partner's phone directly, by QR code or invite link, and is never sent to the database. Whoever operates the servers cannot read your notes. Nor can we.

What the database can see

Encryption hides content, not the shape of it. Stored unencrypted are: the two anonymous identifiers sharing a board, how many notes exist, and when they were created or changed. Your chosen fridge colour stays on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

The encryption key, your fridge colour, which notes you have seen, and the text of any reminder waiting to be shown. Reminder text is held in plain form by Android's own notification system and appears on your lock screen when a reminder fires, so a note's text can be read there by anyone holding the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If both phones lose the key, the notes can never be read again, by anyone.

What we don't do

No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No profiling. Nothing sold or shared with anyone. The app asks for no camera, location, contacts or storage permission; scanning an invite uses the system scanner, which returns only the scanned text.

Services

Notes sync through Google Firebase (anonymous authentication and Firestore), which stores the encrypted data described above. Google's handling of it is covered by their own privacy policy.

Children

FridgeNote is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

The person who started a fridge can delete it from the app, and that removes every note on it and the fridge itself from the database, permanently, for everyone in it. Anyone else can leave a fridge, which removes them from it and erases everything about it from their own phone; the notes stay for the people still using it.

Uninstalling removes everything held on your phone, including the key, but does not delete notes already sent to the database. If you have lost access and want a fridge removed, use the contact below.
