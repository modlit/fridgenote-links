Privacy

FridgeNote · last updated 18 September 2026

FridgeNote is a shared note board for two people. This page describes everything it stores and everything it sends anywhere.

No account, no identity

There is no sign-up. The app creates an anonymous Firebase account, which is a random identifier with no name, email or phone number attached. We never ask for any of those and could not link the app to you if we wanted to.

Your notes are encrypted before they leave the phone

Note text and the display names you choose are encrypted on your device with a key generated on your phone. The key is shared with your partner's phone directly, by QR code or invite link, and is never sent to the database. Whoever operates the servers cannot read your notes. Nor can we.

What the database can see

Encryption hides content, not the shape of it. Stored unencrypted are: the two anonymous identifiers sharing a board, how many notes exist, and when they were created or changed. Your chosen fridge colour stays on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

The encryption key, your fridge colour, and the text of any reminder waiting to be shown. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. If both phones lose the key, the notes can never be read again, by anyone.

What we don't do

No analytics. No advertising. No tracking. No profiling. Nothing sold or shared with anyone. The app asks for no camera, location, contacts or storage permission; scanning an invite uses the system scanner, which returns only the scanned text.

Services

Notes sync through Google Firebase (anonymous authentication and Firestore), which stores the encrypted data described above. Google's handling of it is covered by their own privacy policy.

Children

FridgeNote is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone, of any age.

Contact
